# Medical Disclaimer

**NAVI is a healthcare *navigation* tool. It is not a medical provider, and nothing in
NAVI constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.**

- NAVI helps you understand insurance coverage, find in-network providers, estimate
  costs, and track prior-authorization status for care your own doctor has already
  recommended. It does not recommend, diagnose, or treat any medical condition.
- Information NAVI provides about coverage, cost, or authorization status is based on
  available data at the time of your request and, where noted in the product, may be
  **simulated** rather than a live determination from your insurer — always confirm
  time-sensitive or high-stakes information (e.g. whether a claim will actually be
  paid) directly with your insurance company or provider before making a decision.
- If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency
  number) or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not use NAVI in an emergency.
- NAVI's automated systems, including any AI agents, are designed to escalate to a
  human when they identify a request involving clinical judgment, and are instructed
  not to provide diagnoses or treatment recommendations. If you believe NAVI has
  given you information that resembles medical advice, do not act on it — consult a
  licensed healthcare provider instead, and please report it to `[support email]`.
